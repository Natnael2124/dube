## Full description

**Limit:** 4,000 characters

```
Dube (ድቤ) is an offline credit ledger for retail shopkeepers. Record goods sold on credit, follow who still owes you, and see each customer’s balance — even when there is no internet.

Built for the way local shops actually work: a customer takes sugar, oil, or teff today and pays later. Dube replaces the paper notebook with a clear, searchable book that stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Record a new Dube in seconds — customer name, phone, items, amount, and due date
• See total active credit, overdue accounts, and how many borrowers you have
• Open any customer to view outstanding balance, open debts, and payment history
• Take partial payments or mark a Dube as paid in full
• Extend a due date when you agree to give more time
• Filter the list: all, active, overdue, or settled
• Search by name, phone, or items
• Check a customer’s reliability before you extend more credit (on-time settlements, overdue counts, lifetime borrowed vs repaid)
• Send an SMS reminder through your phone’s own messaging app
• Work fully offline — your books do not need a signal

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE

Your merchant financial ledger is stored only on the device. Dube does not upload customer names, phone numbers, debt amounts, or payment history to a remote server. There is no cloud account to create. Uninstalling the app removes the local database.

Made for shopkeepers who want a simple debt book, not a complicated accounting suite.

ADS

Dube is free to use. It may show Google AdMob banners and local sponsor cards. Advertising does not read your ledger. See the in-listing Privacy Policy for details.

Dube — keep every customer’s credit, due date, and balance in one place, offline.
```
